# KyrooS
**Developed by KonekoDev (Andi)**

KyrooS is a powerful, uncompromising system optimization and hardware tuning utility designed for Non-Rooted Android devices. Built strictly around an **"On-Demand / Hit & Run"** philosophy, KyrooS executes deep system-level optimizations via Shizuku without leaving any battery-draining background services behind. 

---

✨ Key Features

🚀 **On-Demand System Profiles**
Instantly shift your device's behavior. Choose between **Balanced, Powersave, Gaming, or Extreme** profiles. KyrooS dynamically rewrites `activity_manager`, `app_standby`, `device_idle`, and `job_scheduler` constants to match your exact needs on the fly.

🛡️ **Brutal AppOps & Driver Overrides**
Take absolute control over user apps. Aggressively strip background permissions, wake-locks, and foreground services using **Brutal AppOps**. Furthermore, force specific apps to use **ANGLE, Game, or Prerelease GPU drivers** with a single tap.

🧹 **Deep Telemetry & Logger Cleaning**
Upon first setup, KyrooS obliterates system bloat by disabling aggressive tracking. It halts `looper_stats`, media metrics, binder tracking, window manager tracing, and background telemetry to free up CPU cycles and RAM.

⚡ **Zero Background Bloat (Hit & Run Execution)**
Unlike other optimizers, KyrooS does not monitor your device in the background. Optimizations are compiled into temporary shell scripts, executed securely via Shizuku, and immediately deleted. Zero persistent memory footprint.

---

📖 How to Use
1. **Install** the KyrooS APK.
2. **Prerequisite:** Ensure you have the [Shizuku](https://shizuku.rikka.app/) app installed and its service running.
3. **Setup:** Open KyrooS and grant the requested Shizuku permission.
4. **Optimize:** Navigate to the "Config" tab, select your desired **System Profile**, and let KyrooS do the magic. (You can also explore specific app tweaks in the "Apps" tab).

---

📱 Compatibility

· **Android Version:** Supports Android 7.0 (Nougat) and higher (API 24+).
· **Architecture:** Optimized for all modern Android architectures (ARM, ARM64, x86).
· **Requirement:** A functional Shizuku environment is strictly required for full feature access.

---

🔒 Permissions & Privacy

**Respecting Your Privacy**
KyrooS is fully open and locally executed. No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted over the internet.

**Essential Permissions**
· **Shizuku API:** Used for secure, elevated access (ADB-level) to bypass standard Android restrictions and inject system settings.
· **Write Secure Settings:** Allows modification of hidden global system variables and GPU driver selections.
· **Notifications:** To notify you exactly when a profile has been successfully applied to the system.

---

🌟 Benefits

· **Maximum Standby Time:** The Powersave profile forces extreme doze parameters and cuts background checks, significantly extending battery life.
· **Peak Gaming Performance:** The Gaming & Extreme profiles lift thermal limits, prioritize CPU allocation, and enforce hardware rendering.
· **Cooler Device:** By killing unnecessary loggers and background syncs, your SoC works less during idle times.
